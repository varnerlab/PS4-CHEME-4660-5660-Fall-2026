Problem Set 4: Does Selling Options Pay?

Release: Sunday, October 25, 2026
Initial submission deadline: Sunday, November 8, 2026, at 11:59 PM ET
Maximum score: 4 points

When you sell an option, you collect a premium today. If it expires in the money, you pay the buyer the difference between the share price and the strike, which can be many times the premium. Buying a second option farther from the share price caps that loss, but costs part of the premium. In PS4 you replay 174 historical trades on 29 stocks and funds, each held about a month, with four structures: a short straddle, a short strangle, a protected strangle (which also buys a call), and an iron condor (which also buys a call and a put). You ask whether each made a profit as often as the option prices implied, and whether its return on capital beat cash, SPY, and the stock itself. The Advanced track adds four one-sided structures to show which side made or lost the money. You are graded on correct calculations and interpretation, never on which structure does best.

Check your work

In VS Code, open the extracted folder containing Project.toml, README.md, and check_submission.jl. Open a terminal in that folder and run this setup command once: 

julia --project=. --startup-file=no -e 'using Pkg; Pkg.instantiate()'

After saving your code, run this command to check it and generate the report:

The checker runs the 24 Standard or 40 Advanced checks that the rubric scores.
